# Sum Ltd Automation: "Beads" Feature Pipeline

## Phase 1: Intelligent Contact Matrix (NOW)
**Goal:** Automate email recipient logic to remove "CC Paranoia".
*   In Settings, create a grid where you assign `To:` and `CC:` addresses for each **Business Entity** (LC Solar 1, 2, etc.) and **Job Category** (Groundworks, Survey).
*   Logic: Clicking "Send Invoice" automatically populates the correct list based on the job being sent.

## Phase 2: Branded PDF Engine
**Goal:** Professional document generation.
*   "Download PDF" button for Quotes, Subbie POs, and Client Invoices.
*   Includes brand logo, dynamic MW totals, and standard terms.

## Phase 3: Storage & Folder Automation
**Goal:** Organized file management.
*   Implement the `Site > Year > Process` logic in a **Document Archive** view.
*   Prepare the architecture for real OneDrive/Google Drive sync.

## Phase 4: Subcontractor Self-Service Portal
**Goal:** Remove manual data entry for completion.
*   Secure links for Lemp/Sitemark to upload their own invoices and report links.

## Phase 5: Financial Intelligence (VAT & Forecasting)
**Goal:** Business planning.
*   Real-time VAT liability tracking for quarterly returns.
*   Profitability forecasting based on open operations.
