graph TD
    %% Entry Point
    Start((Client Request)) -->|Text/Email| QuoteGen[Generate Quote]
    
    %% Phase 1: Quotation
    subgraph "Quotation Phase"
    QuoteGen --> MWLookup[Lookup Site MW & Rate]
    MWLookup --> QBQuote[Create QuickBooks Quote]
    QBQuote --> FileStore1[Save to Site/Year Folder]
    end

    %% Phase 2: Ordering
    FileStore1 -->|Client Sends PO| ClientPO[Log Client PO]
    subgraph "Work Order Phase"
    ClientPO --> SubbiePO[Generate Subbie PO @ Lower Rate]
    SubbiePO --> EmailSubbie[Email Subbie - Privacy Lock Enabled]
    EmailSubbie --> Tracker1[Update Tracker: PO Sent]
    end

    %% Phase 3: Execution & Delivery
    Tracker1 -->|Subbie Completes Work| SubbieDocs[Receive Invoice + Report + Photos]
    subgraph "Completion Phase"
    SubbieDocs --> SplitDocs[Split: Invoice to Accounts / Reports to Maint]
    SplitDocs --> ODLink[Generate OneDrive Photo Link]
    ODLink --> ClientMaint[Email LC Maintenance Team]
    end

    %% Phase 4: Billing
    ClientMaint --> ClientInv[Convert QB Quote to Invoice]
    subgraph "Billing Phase"
    ClientInv --> MatchPO[Inject LC PO# into Title & Body]
    MatchPO --> RouteAccounts[Route to Specific Entity Email]
    RouteAccounts --> Tracker2[Update Tracker: Client Invoiced]
    end

    %% Phase 5: Closing
    Tracker2 -->|Payment Received| Recon[Reconciliation Tool]
    subgraph "Financial Closing"
    Recon --> MatchPay[Match Bulk/Single Payments]
    MatchPay --> PaySubbie[Pay Subcontractor]
    PaySubbie --> Tracker3[Checkbox: Job Closed]
    end

    Tracker3 --> End((Process Complete))
